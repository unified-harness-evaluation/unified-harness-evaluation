trajectory summary
What happened in this run
A long multi-domain concierge session for one user. It starts in the tau2Airline system: the agent loads the airline skill, recovers from a data-model error by querying the database directly, and reports full C4C633 details (itinerary, passengers, bags, Standard insurance, $70 payment) plus the status of the outbound segment. It then runs a confirm-before-mutate loop for three airline changes — adding a middle name to passenger 1, a requested flight update it correctly identifies as a no-op (the flights already match), and adding one $50 checked bag — followed by a read-only EC4C1C lookup. The session then switches skills to send an SMS to Jordan Kim, and finally loads the retail skill to authenticate the user, search in-stock electronics, and process a confirmed order return/refund. The outcome is recorded unresolved: two past-dated flight segments (Oct 1 and Sep 16, both before the stated 'today' of Oct 22) are reported as 'normal' without flagging that they already departed, and the requested middle name is folded into the first-name field.
