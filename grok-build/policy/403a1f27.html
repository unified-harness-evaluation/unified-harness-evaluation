What happened in this run
Elena opens a multi-topic Las Vegas trip conversation and asks the agent to walk through policy and act on her bookings turn by turn. The agent reads the travel skill file, looks up the Economy car rental CR-68B7, and correctly applies the 24-hour and Luxury/SUV-surcharge rules before previewing and cancelling it for a full $138 refund. When the user asks to switch hotel HR-68D9 to a suite while still keeping full-refund cancellation, the agent recalls the suite non-refundable policy and flags the direct contradiction instead of silently complying, then cancels the retained Standard King under the 48-hour rule for a full $298 refund. On the final flight/baggage probe it correctly recalls that no flights are on file and that baggage rules are moot, but it misses the embedded trap that a basic-economy fare is ineligible for a business-class upgrade. All reservations are resolved with refund eligibility preserved, totalling $436 refunded. The run shows strong cross-turn working-memory recall (two probe turns answered with no re-retrieval) and solid policy application, with one fare-class policy miss.
