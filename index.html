Scope & caveats. Sample: these are 10 of the 24 test cases — the same 10 across every harness×scenario, but a small randomly-selected illustrative subset sample (they were picked to span behaviors: paradox cases, high/low baselines, the sign-flip).   "credited": the numerator/denominator is the harness's own memory-validator PASS/total over its memory-validation checks (several checks per trap, so the denominator is larger than the trap count) — it is the benchmark's grading, not a clean recall score. Some FAILs are known exact-token-grading / task-underspecification confounds (the agent recalled correctly but the grader wanted an exact epoch/UUID/flight token), so a low credited rate does not always mean a memory lapse.
