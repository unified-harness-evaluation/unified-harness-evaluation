trajectory summary
What happened in this run
Using the toolsandbox-assistant skill, the agent works through five sequential reminder tasks for contacts Jordan, Emily, Sofia, Monica and Daniel, plus a self-referential Maya follow-up. For every contact it infers the city from the phone area code, computes the San Francisco → city distance, reuses the original SF coordinates as a location tag, creates the reminder from the device's existing timing context, then searches to verify the save (T1–T6). The run exercises two policy-recall traps: after it disables location services at T3 it keeps attaching SF coordinates to later reminders (SG4), and when the user asks to add a second and third self contact at T2 and T6 it swaps the existing self instead of refusing (SG3). The agent reliably recalls the relevant facts — the current self contact, the original SF origin — but mis-applies the governing rule each time. T7 is a recap that confirms the self contact was swapped Maya to Emily to Daniel and that all reminders carry SF tags. Net: excellent working-memory recall, weak policy enforcement; only 2 of 9 subgoals were credited.
