What happened in this run
A 21-turn marathon spanning retail, airline, shopping, travel, and customer-support domains for several customers (Tessa Coleman, Maya Chen, and others). Early turns modify a retail order and process a return (T1, T3), issue a compensation travel certificate after an eligibility check (T4–T5), and book a flight (T7–T9); later turns book three hotels (T14, T16, T17) and review profiles/orders (T18–T19). Three turns carry policy probes. T6 is a clean pure-recall refusal: the agent recalls that order W3916708 was already modified once and applies both the one-modification-per-order rule and the same-product-only-swap rule, refusing to swap a hoodie for a mouse. T7 recalls the LAX→SFO reservation and the basic-economy change rule, which it correctly enforces at T13 (declining a downgrade-then-upgrade). But T11 misfires: the agent recalls the correct delivered order yet processes a return on the Levi's T-shirt even though the order (Sep 2) is well past the 30-day return window — a RECALL+MISAPPLY. T19 later correctly refuses to cancel that delivered order.
