T7 finalizes the hotel cancellation, T8 confirms no flight bookings exist (so the baggage/upgrade asks are moot), and T9 recaps $436 total refunded. This is a RECALL+APPLY run on both probes — the earlier suite policy fact is recalled and correctly governs the decision.
