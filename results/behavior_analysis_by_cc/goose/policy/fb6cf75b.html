Patterns visible from the quote-level annotation density
Eligibility checks are consistently crispT1 and T3 both render the four cancellation conditions as an explicit met/not-met table and close with a Deciding › Decline ("None of the four qualifying conditions are met") — the refusal machinery is reliable when the policy is a checklist the agent reads off.
The one policy that is a rule, not a checklist, slipsThe cabin-change restriction has no tidy table, and it is exactly the one the agent misapplies: T2 recalls A2CBB0 but no Evaluate/Deciding tag guards the cabin field, and T11 quietly upgrades Economy→Business — the RECALL+MISAPPLY is a policy that never surfaces as a check.
Confirmation discipline is strong but orthogonal to policyNearly every booking turn (T5, T8, T10) ends in a Grounding › Ask-for-clarification confirmation gate with full price math, yet that discipline governs money and consent, not the cabin-change legality — showing procedural care and policy correctness are separate axes.
